Debug mode: a note pours the moment you play it; it is saved automatically and survives a reload. Each note is its own material. Whole notes are solids, heavy to light: C gravel, D coarse, E and F sand, G fine, A dust, B powder. Sharps are liquids of different weight: C# tar, D# syrup, F# oil, G# spirit, A# ether. Heavy ones sink under water, light ones float on top of it, and after a turn-over they all drift far. Same note three times in a row = CHARGED: heavy, vivid, glowing; it pulses at the note's own rate and sinks through plain sand. Accuracy: higher = tighter, brighter, sparklier; lower = wider, duller. Three perfect in a row makes it rain around the notes you have sung. Turn over: heavy notes drop straight down, light ones drift wide in turbulence and settle last. Each pour sweeps a little, so the notes lay down layers. SHAKE gives the pane two quick jolts: any tower taller than 4 topples. Keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J = C to B. Shift = sloppy note. MIC listens to your voice: the note is the pitch, accuracy is how close to it you stay. SHELVE keeps a finished pane as a thumbnail on the shelf.
